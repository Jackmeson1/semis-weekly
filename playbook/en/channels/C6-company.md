# C6 Companies and earnings

Layer: L6 · Main horizon: mid (one earnings cycle)

## Explainer · Added

- In the author's ranking the company layer never comes first and has a say only within one earnings cycle (HZ-03). Its role is to **test** the questions raised in C5: what return the hyperscalers' capex is actually earning, and whether the chip companies have capacity and pricing power.
- **Assets with solid fundamentals**: companies whose revenue grows on real demand rather than being pushed up by liquidity. The author believes such assets are not sensitive to the level of long-end rates (NOVETO-01).
- **Long-term anchor**: positioning and rates can pull prices off course in the short run, but over more than one earnings cycle prices return to earnings. The research has 13 records of this relationship.

## What the author thinks

- Earnings come into focus only after the far horizon gives the all-clear: the author noted that S&P earnings expectations had been revised up 5% since the conflict began, and that the next thing to watch is whether AI demand delivers, so the focus returns to earnings.
- If earnings show no landmines, turn to macro: the author said no landmines were visible in the results, so one should pay more attention to macro, where the next problems lie.
- SW-m-04 stock-picking method: once the sector has stabilized, pick companies with very strong results whose share price has fallen below its pre-earnings level.

## Signals

There is no automatic signal in v1. The inputs are:
- The earnings calendar for this week and next (`sources/calendar.yaml`)
- Key points from reports released this week: revenue, gross margin, capex guidance, management's comments on demand (compiled by hand in v1, or taken from 8-K press releases)

## Common pitfalls

- Do not treat "good results, falling share price" as an opportunity automatically. Check C4 first: if positioning is driving the selling, wait for positioning to repair (SW-m-01).
- A semiconductor company's own EPS is not the most important thing; the hyperscalers' answers matter more.

## What to write in this week's log

State (whether there were important earnings this week), one line, evidence, the answer to C5's question (adds / subtracts / no information), deviations and uncertainty.
