# C5 Industry

Layer: L5 · Main horizon: far (six months or more)

## Explainer · Added

- **The demand test for semiconductors sits downstream**: a chip company's revenue ultimately depends on how much capex the hyperscalers are willing to spend. So to judge the direction of semiconductors, first look at how much customers are spending and how long they can afford to keep doing it.
- **TSMC monthly revenue**: published before the 10th of each month, it is the earliest first-hand reading on the whole AI chip chain, one to three months ahead of quarterly reports.
- **Monthly revenue of the Taiwan AI server chain**: assembly (Hon Hai, Quanta, Wistron, Wiwynn), power and cooling (Delta Electronics), and packaging and testing (ASE), which reflect the pace of server shipments.
- **Look at the change in the change**: if capex growth year over year falls from +60% to +30%, the absolute level is still rising but growth is slowing. Share prices often react first to a change in growth rate.

## What the author thinks

- Rule four, earnings are a test of the hyperscalers: the author holds that the issue is not earnings expectations (apart from TSLA and AAPL, the results this round will not look bad) but that the hyperscalers have to answer what return their capex is actually earning.
- INV-03: semiconductors are a purely supply-driven industry; once the big manufacturers decide to add capacity, the broad picture of strong demand and shortages does not change.
- Where hyperscaler capex comes from: the author argued that hyperscalers are not spending based on orders from Anthropic or OpenAI, but responding to demand from their own enterprise customers, which still exceeds supply.
- In 2026 the author repeatedly said memory is a commodity cycle and does not deserve a growth valuation; once memory price increases peak, gross margin peaks too.

## Common pitfalls

- Monthly revenue is in Taiwan dollars, so exchange rates affect the year-over-year figure.
- Not all of TSMC's revenue is AI; server-chain companies also have non-AI businesses.
- Not all hyperscaler capex goes to AI chips; some goes to land, buildings and networking. Finance leases are not counted in v1.
- This channel is slow. Do not over-read a single month's move; look for continuity.
